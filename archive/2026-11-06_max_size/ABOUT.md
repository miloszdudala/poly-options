# Archived proof book: bets as large as the order book allowed (6-18 November 2026)

The bets the model recorded from 6 November 2026 until the morning of
18 November 2026. Each bet was as large as the order book allowed while it
still cleared the bar.

On 18 November the model changed to one share of every bet, and a new book
started in `proof/`. This one is kept for the record but is no longer part of
the proof. Bets still open here are settled in this folder's SETTLEMENTS.md
and PERFORMANCE.md; no new bets are added.
