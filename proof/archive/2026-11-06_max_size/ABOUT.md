# Book 1: max size, 2c YES premium (6-18 November 2026)

The bets the `mvp` model recorded from 6 November 2026 until the morning of
18 November 2026. Each bet was as large as the order book allowed while it
still cleared the bar, and every YES bet was charged a 2c risk premium first.

On 18 November the model changed to one share of every bet and no YES
premium, and a new book started in `proof/`. Bets still open here are settled
in this folder's SETTLEMENTS.md and PERFORMANCE.md; no new bets are added.
