How the models bet

Every model looks at the same priced markets of one type each cycle and decides which bets to buy, from offers that already clear its bar. This page explains the rule a bet is judged by and lets you try it on your own numbers. The calculator runs the same arithmetic as models/base.py.

1. A bet

Every market is a barrier on a stock before a deadline, for example "Will EWY reach $194 by Friday?" (a hit_up market) or "Will TSLA fall to $367.50?" (a hit_down market). Each market has two sides. A share pays $1 if its side wins and $0 if it loses.

How a bet is judged pipeline/offers.py · Bar, Bet
  • cost = best ask + Polymarket's fee, 0.04 × price × (1 − price) per share.
  • value = the win probability, minus a 2¢ risk premium on a YES (approved 2026-10-09; INSIGHTS §7.2).
  • EV = value − cost per share, and ROI = EV ÷ cost.
  • Bar (today's, TODAYS_BAR; Open in CLAUDE.md): a bet clears if EV ≥ 3¢ and ROI ≥ 8%. Above a 90¢ ask the bar is EV ≥ 0 and ROI ≥ 2%.
  • Limit: the harness buys deeper levels of the book only while each still clears the bar, so every share bought is a good one. The limit is min(value − EV bar, value ÷ (1 + ROI bar)).

2. One cycle

Each cycle the replay (replay.py) builds the offers, every bet of the type that clears the model's bar, best return first, each with max_usd, the most of the book that still clears the bar. It hands them to model.decide(offers, book) with the model's book (open and settled holdings, dollars per direction and per vol, the cash its account allows). The model returns orders.

  • Depth: at most half of each price level, and only levels whose all-in cost is under the bet's limit.
  • One entry per market: a market already held, or held until it settled, is never offered again.
  • Minimum: fills under $1 (Polymarket's minimum order) are dropped.
  • Accounts belong to the harness: max (no ceiling, the routine one), compound and budget. There are no per-market caps.

3. The models

edge_taker (models/edge_taker.py) takes every offer, as large as the book allows. It applies no portfolio rule, on purpose: it measures whether the mispricing exists after costs, not whether a sensible book can be built from it.

random_control (models/random_control.py) has no signal: 12 bets a cycle drawn from every priced bet, at most 2¢ over the best ask. If it earns what edge_taker earns, the window drifted.

The two hedged books that will balance direction, and direction plus volatility, are the user's to design. Their predecessors direction_balanced and scenario_hedged were deleted on 2026-11-04; git history has them and the earlier version of this page.
