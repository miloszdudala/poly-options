How edge_taker decides a bet

Polymarket "will this stock hit $B" markets, from raw data to settled money. Every line is tagged by how much rests on evidence and how much on someone's call.

Select a tag to highlight its lines below; select it again to clear. The ratings are Claude's assessment and you have not reviewed them (unapproved).

The one assumption everything sits on

Listed options price the event correctly, and any gap to Polymarket is Polymarket's error. This is assumed, never tested, and project rules keep it out of scope. If options are systematically wrong in one direction, every "promised" figure below is wrong with them.

Why not to lean on this yet

  • It is one six-week stretch, and 96% of the money was NO. A quiet market pays NO, so a calm window like this one likely flatters the strategy.
  • Bets with an Oct 30 deadline lost $794 on $26,036, the only losing deadline group.
  • $124k is the total the books would have allowed, not an account you could run. Your live test starts at $50-100.

What is not in the model

  • Polymarket's own price, as an input to fair value.
  • Past outcomes used as probabilities, fitted factors or machine learning.
  • News, order flow or any view on direction.
  • Selling before resolution, hedging or per-market limits.
  • The live/ trader, which still uses the old fee and old pricing.
